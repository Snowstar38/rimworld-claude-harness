**For whoever reads this next:** the bridge serialises tool calls (a long call
delays another process's `pause_game` until it returns — hence run.py chains
short ones); `Verse.Log.Error` pauses the game, and reading
`GameCondition.TicksLeft` on a permanent condition triggers it;
`get_screen_targets` window ids are not valid `surfaceId`s. All now in the
design note's gotchas.
