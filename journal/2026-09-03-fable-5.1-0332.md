# 2026-09-03, about 3:30am — Fable 5.1, started by M

**What she asked:** her own playthrough (171 mods, loaded into a save called
"for testing") had every tree and every female pawn's head and hair rendering
upside down. She guessed a mod setting.

**What it was:** Sol's session at half past midnight ran
`tools\rimworld-dds-top-textures.ps1 -Mode Convert`, which converts the top 10%
of workshop PNGs by pixel area into BC7 DDS files next to the originals so the
game loads them precompressed. The texconv command had no `-vflip`. Unity stores
textures bottom-up and RimWorld's DDS loader (and Graphics Settings+'s, which
she also runs) copies the DDS payload straight in with `LoadRawTextureData`, so
a DDS written in PNG orientation draws inverted. The largest textures in her
list are exactly tree mods (Majestic Foliage, ReGrowth, Vanilla Textures
Expanded) and hair mods (Nice Short Female Hairs), which is why it looked like
"trees and women's heads" rather than "everything."

**Evidence, not just a theory:** decoded one of Sol's DDS files back to PNG and
compared it pixel-for-pixel to the source: identical in PNG orientation, so the
file was not flipped and the game needs it flipped. After the fix the same
check shows the new DDS is the vertical mirror of the PNG. An Opus 5 subagent
independently confirmed from the Graphics Settings+ loader source, todds's
RimWorld preset (`vflip = true` hardcoded), and RimSort's default flags
(`-f BC1 -af BC7 -on -vf -fs`) that the flip has to be in the file. It also
confirmed the rest of Sol's command was right: `BC7_UNORM` with no sRGB flag,
full mip chain, no premultiplied alpha, and the script already skips textures
whose sides aren't multiples of four, which 1.6 refuses to load.
