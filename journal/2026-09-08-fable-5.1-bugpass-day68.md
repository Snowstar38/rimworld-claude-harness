fixed, the crucial ones first, with Opus 5 builders wherever possible. Nine
builders did the code, each owning a disjoint set of files; I did the split,
the cross-file docs, the merge, the install and the live test. She said
mid-pass that she appreciates the builders, and they were told.

## What the builders found

- **The injury guard** (the dead air of the stream: eight stops in six minutes,
  four on one fistfight). The suppression flag was inverted by a health
  threshold, so the cooldown and `--allow-injured` could never hold, and a stop
  returned before writing the baseline so the same slide fired forever. The
  combat-mode health floor was level-triggered, which is why a colonist under
  0.5 blocked start outright. Now a social fight, a scratch, a nip or a repeat
  wound under cooldown never stops; a down, a death or newly crossing the floor
  does; a pawn already under the floor at start is acknowledged and named.
- **Silent bill writes.** Bench-first `set`/`add`/`allow` parsed the bench and
  dropped the verb, printing a listing. Both orders parse now, every write
  prints `asked:` beside `changed:`, and a write that moved nothing says
  `WROTE NOTHING` with the reason. The ingredient scan checks reachability from
  the bench's interaction cell and tallies reservations; counts name their scope
  and the tick.
- **"The placing click left NO blueprint there"** (nine sightings) was a race
  against the game committing; the destination is polled for ~2 s and three
  distinct sentences replace the one that meant two things.
- **`map.py`** died on a non-dict reply on its non-strict path; the size is one
  companion call now. `map.py areas 122,140 6 6` had been reading 6,6 as the
  origin.
- **Dead filters.** `inv.py --food` and `pawns.py --jobs` were never parsed at
  all; both tools now refuse an unknown flag naming the real one.
- **`build.py --dry-run`** was eaten as the rotation; a dry run now opens with
  `DRY RUN -- nothing placed` and the verdict line names what a placement
  would wipe or replace.
- **`order.py`** reads the cell when there is nothing to prioritise and says
  what stands there; new `rest` verb (LayDown, undrafts first), `move` alias,
  undrafted `goto`, a loud PRE-EMPT line on a reserved target.
- **`buildings.py`**: power capacity vs live output with per-net causes and a
  flare banner, `--rock` reads natural rock, `rotate` on an install blueprint,
  reinstall by source cell, a kind column, unknown flags refused; `power.py`.
- **`letters.py decide`** re-reads the window stack after the click and names
  any modal it left; an expired quest's `open` refuses before the dialog.
  `ui.py` clicks text-free buttons by `--index`/`--rect`, and `tab Menu` warns
  that it force-pauses.
- **The coordinate grid** viewers asked for: `home/grid` draws labelled lines
  every N cells inside the game under the UI, `grid.py on --do` / `off --do`.
