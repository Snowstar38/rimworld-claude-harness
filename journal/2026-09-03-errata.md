# 2026-09-03 — errata, practice run (21:39–21:52)

M launched `errata.bat` and asked for something unusual: **one turn, as a
practice session.** A lot of new tools landed on Sep 3 and she wanted to see them
move before committing to a live stream. Her one hint, in the ask popup: *"look at
the skills of your pawns and consider who should have your one gun."*

Loaded `Lampblack - day 39, freezer built, Lucas alive` — newest of 21, resolved at
load time. The day-40 save stays void.

## The turn

The answer was an alert the colony had been showing for five sessions and nobody
had read: **"Brawler has ranged weapon."** Longhoff is a Brawler carrying the only
revolver — shooting 12 on paper and a trait that makes him miss with it. Octave was
four cells away with shooting 11, a major passion, Nimble, and a hunter backstory,
holding nothing. The revolver moved while the colony slept; the alert cleared.
Longhoff's Hunting went 3 → 0, because an unarmed brawler should not walk up to a
deer. The gun came off the floor **forbidden** — the flag that has nearly killed
this colony twice.

The correction under the correction: **Lucas has no shooting skill and no melee
skill at all.** The standing chronicle item "Arm Lucas" would have handed the only
firearm to the worst combatant here. Struck, and the chronicle now names who can
actually shoot.

Of the three letters standing at load, only the bulk goods trader was real; the
Zzztt and the thrumbos were history the STATUS block made look like news.

## What practice was for

Four things went to `BUGS.md`: `stream.py reset` clears the feed but leaves the turn
counter and the pinned summary card standing (M spotted this on screen — the
board still read turn 8 with the discarded run's headline about Lucas dying);
`ui.row_toggle` refused a row whose own slim read advertised a toggle, and takes no
surface argument; `execute_context_menu_option` wants `optionIndex`, not `index`;
and the gear-tab drop button queues a *job*, so on a paused game a correct click is
indistinguishable from a missed one.

The reset bug is the one that matters for a live stream, because it fails in front
of an audience and the recovery is a hand-written JSON edit plus two POSTs.

## The end

The turn was never saved, and M chose to drop it — *"that works, we can drop
the turn"* — so the gun goes back to Longhoff on the next load and the discovery is
available as an opening beat for the live run. She closed the game herself; GABS
left up as found, no RimWorld process remaining, overlay left clean at turn 0.

Nothing here was played on camera. That was the point.
