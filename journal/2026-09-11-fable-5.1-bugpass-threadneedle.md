# 2026-09-11 — Fable 5.1, the Threadneedle bug pass

M asked for the bugs and wanted lists to be fixed, the Threadneedle
night's sixteen first, with Opus 5 builders wherever possible. Ten builders did
the code in parallel, each owning a disjoint set of files; a doc integrator
applied their PLAYBOOK / INSTALL / BUGS / WANTED text; I did the split, the
merge, the build, the install and the live test. She was away from the PC and
said live testing was fine, so the game was up for most of it.

## What the builders found

- **The turret pack** (the one that got away on Sep 8). A pack deploy is a
  thrown grenade, not a build order: `Verb_LaunchProjectileStaticOneUse`,
  range 22.9, line of sight required. A cell holding any Building (a conduit
  under the floor counts) swallows the click and leaves the targeter open; a
  cell out of line of sight closes the targeter and places nothing. Neither
  says a word, and from inside a mined-out mountain room the far side of the
  rock is invisible however close. `Apparel_PackTurret` is Anomaly, not
  Odyssey. New `home/order deploy` runs the gates one at a time and names the
  one that failed, with up to 8 nearby cells that pass; `order.py deploy` is
  the one order verb that is a dry run until `--do`, because the pack destroys
  itself when it fires.
- **The ghoul.** RimWorld's own `Pawn.IsColonist` ends in `!IsSubhuman`, and a
  ghoul's MutantDef is `consideredSubhuman`, so Ben Cooper was `isColonist:
  false` in every payload and every caller dropped him. `home/list_pawns` rows
  carry `ghoul` and `playerFaction` now; the rule for "ours" is `isColonist or
  (ghoul and playerFaction)`, one copy in `pawns.is_colony_member`. The roster
  lists him, and `combat.py begin` sweeps `home/list_pawns` so `draft Ben`
  works. That was also the root of "not one unambiguous colonist (0 matches)".
- **The wolverine.** The supervised-play guard's entire predator test was job
  name plus a 40-cell radius, and the animal it stopped for appeared in none of
  the lists the refusal printed, so `--ignore-hostile all` acknowledged an
  empty set and the next start refused identically. Four nights. Threats are
  classified now (`manhunter`, `hostile`, `predator_hunting_ours`,
  `predator_near` at 12 cells, and `predator` which does not stop the clock);
  bare `--ignore-hostile` means all; `--ignore-predator` never covers a raid.
- **Skills.** The payload had every skill level all along; the roster threw
  away anything under 5 or without a passion. Every skill prints now, `+`/`++`
  for passion, `-` for never; `pawns.py --skills` is the table with a `best:`
  footer. "Construction skill too low" is Ernst 2, Reikguard 0, Samantha 3.
- **Two pawns on one tile.** `get_map_target_info` nests `DescribePawn`, which
  has `pawnId` and no `thingId`; `pick.resolve` returned that and the gizmo
  reader compared against a blank. Pawns are selected by id through
  `rimworld/select_pawn` now, never by clicking their cell.
- **The targeter is not a Window.** Nothing in either tree read
  `Find.Targeter`; `home/status` carries `ui.targeter` now and `ui.py click`
  reads it back instead of saying "nothing opened or closed".
- **64 cells in one call** was never a batch: one process, one watch lead, one
  probe and one placing call per cell, 1.77 s each, 113 s for the wall.
  `home/place_building` takes `cells` now, 8 placements per main-thread hop
  with a 20 ms gap so letters land mid-batch. A 12-cell wall took 1.88 s live.
- **Verb-less bill writes** fell through to the listing; `say.py` without
  `--mood` dropped the face; `--near` ignored `--radius`; `at` was a substring
  match; `combat.py end` paused before deciding and refused over sleeping
  insectoids 60 cells away; `advance 25` refused and paused; hunt and tame were
  cell designators aimed at a designation that hangs on the animal; the four
  `ConnectionRefused` test errors were one unmocked `clear_designator`.
- WANTED 4 (a line budget for the Scout brief) and 7 (`pawn_config` hunt /
  tame) shipped. WANTED 3 got a twelve-item audit instead of edits.

  real right-click did not close it either. What closed it was the next

## The second round

Five builders took a follow-up each and the DLL was rebuilt, reinstalled with
the game closed, and the stack restarted (24 tools, no warnings). Verified
live on the restart: `id:` on every animal line and `thingId` on every JSON
row; `pawns.py Ibex45901` matched by id; `ui.py targeter --cancel` closing the
targeter by clearing the selection and saying it cost the selection; the
batch rerun reading `ALREADY THERE -- all 12`. The `hostile_dormant` category
(asleep job AND more than 50 cells away) did not fire on the hive, and that
was right: at 18h on day 12 the insectoids read `Wait_Wander` and
`GotoWander`, awake in their cave 74-78 cells out. The guard keeps the
stricter AND rule; bare `--ignore-hostile` is the one-flag acknowledgment.

The combat builder's order-dependent test failure turned out to be a real
leak: `combat.main` armed the live `home/list_pawns` reader and never
disarmed it, so a later unit test adopted a real pawn from the running game.
Cleared in `main`'s `finally`, and the roster tests now assert the reader is
disarmed rather than resetting it.

The docs integrator ran out of Opus session budget halfway through
INSTALL.md; M said the limit had reset and it was resumed.
