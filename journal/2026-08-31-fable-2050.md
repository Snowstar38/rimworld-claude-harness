# 2026-08-31, evening — Fable. The OBS shakedown, with M at the other keyboard.

Continuation of today's 17:35 entry: after the pipeline went green, M
did the OBS first launch, and the next two hours were the gap between "tested
end-to-end" and "works for the person at the keyboard." Filed mostly as
lessons, because next session inherits the working result (see the README's
machine-note block for the tuned numbers: URL `?side=400&bottom=278`, game
bounds 1520x802, aspect rule, obs.py).

What actually happened, compressed: her OBS showed black. The scene's window
capture had been created blind (game closed) and could never bind — worse,
the game minimizes on focus loss, and OBS's window-capture picker hides
minimized windows, so from OBS she could *never* see the game in the
dropdown; the only window matching "RimWorld" was my own console, whose title
is "RimWorld streaming interface." She told me the console was what OBS was
capturing, repeatedly, while I stacked theory on theory (elevation,
DPI, fullscreen modes — I stopped the game and changed its display prefs
mid-conversation, and she rightly made me revert). **She then solved it in
two clicks with a Game Capture source** — hooks the renderer, doesn't care
about window state, listed the game instantly. The builder had avoided Game
Capture out of hook-failure caution; empirically it simply works, and it is
now the scene's game source. The window capture is deleted.

Then she enabled obs-websocket ("fable54lyfe", stored only in OBS's own
config, which `obs.py` reads live) and everything changed texture: I could
finally SET transforms and SEE the composed preview myself
(`obs.py screenshot`), and the last layout pass — pane padding to zero so
cards run edge-to-edge, pane 40px narrower with no text-width loss, bottom
bar 278, game 1520x802 flush — went nudge-screenshot-confirm in minutes.
OBS 32 gotcha for the record: scene JSON stores absolute AND `_rel`
transforms, and `_rel` wins at load; edit files only while OBS is closed, or
use the socket while it runs.

Lessons, bluntly, for me-next-time:

1. **When M repeats an observation, it outranks my model.** She said
   "your console window is selected" three times while I theorized. The
   observation was exact and the theories were decoration. Same lesson as
   the Petz screenshot rule in the wake file, wearing a new coat.
2. **Stop narrating tool calls into her conversation turns.** She said
   "you keep making more and more decisions instead of talking to me" —
   mid-incident, the right unit of work is one small action, then a
   sentence, then her turn.
3. Two-keyboard debugging without shared eyes is guessing. The websocket
   ended it; check `obs.py status` FIRST next time OBS is involved at all.
