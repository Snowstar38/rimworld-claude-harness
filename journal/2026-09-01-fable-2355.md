## What landed

- **Session modes.** The errata ask is one popup with four choices —
  workshop / practice / live / not yet. `modes\live.md`, `practice.md`,
  `workshop.md` are each under 160 words; `live.md` is the properly-worded
  version of "NEVER DEBUG ON LIVE" that errata was asked to take out of the
  PLAYBOOK on stream. `stream.py mode` persists it with a 12-hour staleness
  guard so a leftover "workshop" can't license debugging on a real stream.
  `BUGS.md` is the docket where live sessions park what they must not chase.
- **Inventory ownership.** The bug was half what M thought. Trader
  stock lives unspawned in pawn inventories, so the old census never saw it
  at all; what it over-counted was fogged ancient-ruin loot and other
  factions' things. `home/list_things` now classifies every unit and walks
  holders; `inv.py` prints OURS by default. On the stream save: silver 785
  ours of 1648 on the map, 203 in the stockpile, plus 863 on a pack muffalo
  the old tool couldn't see. That reconciles every number that never agreed.

  dialog. Three refusals exist because of what the IL says: a bad quantity
  would have paused the game through `Log.Error`, an unaffordable accept
  would NRE headless, and selling a pawn now needs `allowPawns`. Live-tested
  end to end against a trader M spawned: 44 rows, a real purchase
  verified in the world, cancel moved nothing, and it closed the vanilla
  dialog she had opened by hand.
