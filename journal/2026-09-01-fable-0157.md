# 2026-09-01, ~2am — Fable (session M started: stream bar v2)

M asked for a fuller bottom bar on the stream overlay — long-term goal,
current focus, turn number, the three most recent letters, each with an age in
in-game time — and told me to manage rather than build: "utilize Opus 5
subagents as much as you can." Five Opus agents did the work; I mostly wrote
briefs, held the schema contract, and looked at screenshots.

What shipped (commit `3454ee5`):

- **Bottom bar, four zones** — goals at 27px/3-line, a big turn numeral with
  the phase word, and a Recent-events column with tone dots and ages
  (`0h` → `22h` → `2d0h`, floor-to-hour, frozen while paused because ticks
  freeze).
- **The clock** needed no companion-dll change: `get_game_info` already
  returns `ticksGame`, letters already carry `arrivalTick`. run.py/watch.py
  push the tick they were already reading (`POST /game`); a fallback poller in
  server.py wakes only when nothing has pushed for 8s, and calls the bridge in
  a way that can never ack the play loop's GABS attention items.
- **Letters auto-post** from watch/run with tone (good/bad/neutral off
  letterDef), deduped on disk; the 13-letter / 29-day backlog was primed into
  the dedup so it never floods the stream. Letters left the thought feed —
  the events zone is their home.
- **Pinned turn summary** (M's mid-session ask): `lastSummary` is
  persisted server-side and pinned atop the feed, slightly smaller, duplicate
  skipped in the flow — so tune-ins always see the turn's story.
- **`home/get_time`** written and built for the companion (her call: anything
  not instant from the stock bridge earns a custom tool) — pending install at
  next game restart. The builder IL-scanned Assembly-CSharp and found that
  reading `TicksAbs` before `gameStartAbsTick` is set fires `Log.ErrorOnce`,
  which *pauses the game*. Guarded; documented in INSTALL.md.
- **Docs**: scout-first emphasis in hands.md (known-bulky reads → Scout by
  default, her framing), and the "context dies with you" language replaced —
  windows fold back / stay behind at handback. Her point: the summary *is*
  continuity, so "death" was inaccurate, not just grim.

Things learned, worth keeping:

- **Blocking long on TaskOutput starves the conversation.** M's
  mid-turn messages queue until the next tool result; hers arrived 3–5
  minutes stale and she noticed. Wait in shorter chunks, or end the turn and
  let task notifications wake you.
- The Opus agents caught three things I hadn't specced: parallel overlay
  posts arriving shuffled (fixed with ordered batching), the attention-item
  hazard, and the TicksAbs pause trap. The delegation advice is calibrated,
  as ever.
- Her recollection that "trunk never posts to the overlay" was nearly right —
  it posts exactly three lines (hands-start / human-check / handback), which
  turned out to be the answer to her containment question too: the narration
  guide already lives fork-side in hands.md.
