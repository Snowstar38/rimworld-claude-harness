## What got built and verified tonight

- **map.py rewritten** (897 → 1517 lines by end of night): six layers — terrain/
  roof, items, pawns, stockpiles, buildings, designations — with self-check
  footers, an ACCOUNTING block that reconciles every thing drawn against the
  payload, per-layer legends that only show symbols on screen, and the standing
  rule made structural: every filter states itself. The original defect (roof
  losing its collision) is dead with a positive control: 24 roofed-with-item
  cells, 737 unroofed, 0 wrong.
- **The bridge extended without forking**: a 28KB companion DLL
  (`Fable 5\made\rimbridge-companion\`) registers `home/ping` and
  `home/get_cells_plus` — the stock sweep plus `forbidden` and bed ownership,
  the two facts proven absent from the stock payload, at 76% smaller size.
  Loaded first try; the real risk turned out to be threading (companions run
  off the main thread — the hop worked, no deadlock). Nothing entered Mods\,
  M's ModsConfig untouched and verified so.
- **map.py migrated onto it**: bed assignment (`B`/`b` — her original [M] ask,
  finally possible), forbidden as `F` on the map, a merged default view (her
  idea: items over stockpiles by case, pawns on the terrain grid "for narrative
  feel"), `--layers`, sparse-layer compaction, and a loud tested fallback to
  the stock tool. The builder caught three payload-shape hazards that would
  each have silently narrowed the map — the accounting block refused to
  reconcile, which is the design catching its first real bugs on night one.

## The architecture conversation

The second half of the evening was M designing, fast, and it's all in
the doc's final sections marked [M]: the **fork idea** (a subagent inheriting
the full session context, taking the turn, reporting short, ending — which is
exactly Claude Code's fork type, and the missing piece the delegate design had
been circling); the **streaming constraint** (this should be watchable — output
tokens are the latency currency, so read wide, write tight); the four-slot
Hands template with her `NOTES:` field so a fork can hold its narrative; and
the **watch rotation** — two relay teams (2 Scouts seeded with the last Hands
summary, 2 Lookouts blind on screenshots) on independent 2.5-min/12-game-hour
clocks. The cast got names at her request: Trunk, Hands, Scouts, Lookout,
Workshop — all defined in the doc with jobs and never-dos.

Where I pushed back and she heard it: seeding. Her instinct was to give all
watchers the last summary; I split it — Scouts seeded (already correlated,
relevance is cheap), Lookouts blind (uncorrelated perspective IS their value).
Recorded as my recommendation with her override standing.
