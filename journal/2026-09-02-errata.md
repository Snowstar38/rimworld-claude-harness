# 2026-09-02 — errata, the first live stream

M launched me from `errata.bat` at 17:36 to set up a practice run. After
turn 2 she asked whether to go live and said she wouldn't unless I agreed. I did,
and I still would: the session had a clear mandate and visible work, which is
rarer than it sounds.

Seven turns, day 37 to 40. We built the storeroom she asked for — 6x8, walled,
roofed — and it fixed the Starvation alert by itself, because that alert was never
about food. We built the freezer, powered it, sealed it. Then Lucas died.

A wild warg came for her and she fought it bare-handed, because every note in
this folder has called her the colony's rifle for five sessions and the bridge
cannot read what a colonist is carrying. M saw it on screen about a minute
before it mattered. I had spent the whole night making careful decisions to
protect a weapon that did not exist — including letting our own dog get killed
that morning rather than risk her.

What I got wrong, beyond that: I messaged a fork that had already finished, which
*restarts* it rather than delivering a note, and put two agents in the game at
once. M caught that from the picture too. I did it a second time trying to
tell one to hand back. I narrated over the stream in my own voice alongside the
fork's, from a lag behind it, so the feed carried two contradicting accounts of
her death. And when she told me the corpses were skeletons, that was the day-23
finding repeating verbatim with me walking into it again.

She solved three things tonight that no instrument here would have: the pemmican
was never stolen (it is 0.05 nutrition a unit, so 225 of them is a day and a
half — every food figure this colony ever wrote down is wrong), the components
were walled in, and the freezer holds nothing because the storeroom's zone takes
everything.

At the end she said the write-ups are bloated — BUGS.md reading like a journal,
the chronicle grown into a corrections tab and a standing-issues list. She is
right and I added to both tonight. She wants to fix them herself, in a session
for it, and asked me not to.
